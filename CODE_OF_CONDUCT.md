# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We expect all participants in this project to uphold this code of conduct through their actions and words.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and inclusive of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Being patient and understanding when giving and receiving criticism
* Focusing on what is best for the community
* Showing empathy towards other community members
* Being open to learning from others

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind
* Offensive comments related to gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, mental illness, neuro(a)typicality, physical appearance, body size, age, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, or other identity markers
* Deliberate misgendering or use of "dead" or rejected names
* Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
* Threats or incitement of violence
* Deliberate intimidation
* Sustained disruption of community spaces (online or in-person)
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community interactions, including but not limited to:

* Project repositories and issues
* Pull requests and code reviews
* Discussions and forums
* In-person and virtual events
* Email communications and chats
* Any official project representation

## Reporting Guidelines

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the project maintainers at [contact information]. 

When reporting, please provide:

* Your contact information
* Names/usernames of those involved
* Description of the incident
* Context and any relevant links or screenshots
* Whether the issue is ongoing

## Investigation and Response

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly. The project team is committed to:

* Maintaining confidentiality of reporters
* Investigating all complaints thoroughly
* Taking appropriate action based on findings
* Providing fair treatment to all parties involved

Potential responses include:

* Private warning or discussion
* Public apology requirement
* Temporary suspension from community spaces
* Permanent removal from the project

## Enforcement

Community members who violate this Code of Conduct may face consequences determined by project leadership. Enforcement is intended to be fair, consistent, and constructive.

## Acknowledgment

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and the [Python Community Code of Conduct](https://www.python.org/community/diversity/), which are resources we deeply respect and recommend.

## Questions?

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct, please reach out to the project maintainers.
